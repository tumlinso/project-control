# PA1 live qualification plan

This is the bounded plan for `PC-PA1-LAB` / live qualification. The package acceptance contract currently contains 40 mandatory cases; each remains `not_run_as_acceptance_case` until an exact scenario receipt is supplied. Existing native CPU gates, source tests, scripted responses, and historical receipts may explain overlap, but they do not satisfy a case by themselves.

The case definitions are copied from `planning/project-assistance-v1/machine/acceptance.json` (SHA-256 `8db435add7f875ec96c5a6758b0a7122029515ea2c61689de43fe6a22e8826d6`). Re-fetch that contract and the current candidate identity before execution. Root owns run `PC-PA1-RUN-1`; this record does not mutate native task state.

## Hard budgets and stop rules

- Initial comparison: at most 12 inquiries total, including baseline, retries, and continuations; at most 900 seconds; at most two parallel model executions. The selected baseline and request-policy variants share those caps.
- Held-out follow-up: at most four inquiries and 300 seconds, with a separately tracked budget after initial candidate selection.
- Lifecycle portion: at most 300 seconds and no inference.
- Optional focus comparison: opt-in only; at most 120 seconds, two roots and 12 turns, and it counts inside the initial inquiry cap. Added foreground wait must stay at or below two seconds; elapsed overhead must stay at or below 10%; evidence cannot degrade. Leave automatic mode off unless every limit is enforceable and measured. Disable it immediately on any violation.
- Maximum warm residency is 900 seconds. End by releasing all inference processes/resources owned by the qualification and recording physical release proof.
- No extensions are planned. An extension needs a new predeclared hypothesis and an explicit separate budget before execution; do not reset counters by relabeling a branch.

Stop on an authority or provenance regression, containment failure, missing resource/permission proof, budget exhaustion, or two consecutive non-improving iterations. Retain partial and negative outcomes. Do not infer production readiness or enable automatic work from this qualification.

## Candidate and evidence identity

Before each run boundary, capture current checkout HEAD and dirty/untracked source manifest; receiver manifest and fingerprint; relevant source, configuration, and fixture hashes; actual selected binary, model/weights, and server identity; exact command and arguments; authorization, lease, and foreground-interlock receipt; process PID/start identity; isolated cache identity; and start/end resource observations. Defer if a concurrent edit prevents a consistent snapshot. Scripted/source receipts, inference, device work, sandbox containment, and installed-runtime lifecycle remain distinct evidence classes.

Every completed row in the final matrix must include a nonzero executed count, exact source identity, actual result, timestamps and elapsed time, citations/provenance, applicable reasoning/prefill/tool/queue/foreground timings, resource release proof, omissions, and limits. A missing, historical, fixture-only, or source-only receipt leaves the case unqualified.
