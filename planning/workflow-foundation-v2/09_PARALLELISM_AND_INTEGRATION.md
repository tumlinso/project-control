# 9. One controller, two authorities, bounded parallelism

There are 24 durable lanes but an initial cap of four concurrent spawned workers, not 24 simultaneously running agents. The root strategic controller launches from Project Control. Each authority has a claimable ordinary COORD task then its aggregate epic. When a single executor cannot hold both project-qualified coordinator seats, a local Skills coordinator acts under the one strategic controller; it is not a foreign native child lane.

The COORD tasks have seat locks and read scope but no prerequisite on final integration. Their completion gates require local I90. The epic follows COORD and relies on native child completion rather than explicit child prerequisites. The package checks both the installed parent-plus-prerequisite DAG and the execution DAG including serial queues, cross receipts and terminal-only closure obligations.

## Phases

Baseline/source/test contracts and donor qualification start first. PC I00 integrates early test hooks. K/V implement and independently prove the relocation; PC I10 is the parity gate. Direct binding B follows and PC I20 releases the recipient candidate for Skills.

Schema S follows the supported bindings. Lifecycle L and coherent snapshots R can fan out after S07; admin recovery E follows L. Shared central-file scopes are explicitly ordered.

Skills then observes it, closes I90 and publishes its final receipt. PC X04/I90 close last. **Do not make Skills I60 wait for PC final, or PC I50 wait for Skills final.**

## Managed workspace preparation

All source-producing lanes are proposed `isolated_merge` producers. Integrators are `exclusive` destinations. The manifest deliberately omits guessed worktree paths, branch bases and provisioning commands. Discover actual supported owner APIs in A02. Provision the destination and correct integration-task/base binding **before any producer must finish**.

Phase progression must preserve the native base-matching rules. The same durable integration lane has serial milestones; choose an explicitly supported base refresh/rebind or a validated new phase lane when needed. Do not copy a predecessor's isolated integrator mistake or paper over a mismatch with direct SQLite.

A task's gate runs in its dispatch source. A dependent must have the qualified producer source available, not just a done flag. Integrate incrementally, resolve ownership transfers through workflow, register tests early, and avoid requiring a test-registration milestone to wait on the tests it enables.

Independent review streams use `specialist` because they author test files; strict `validator` role lacks ordinary edit-scope authority in the inspected role contract. Keep their source scope limited to independent tests/docs and their reviewer identity distinct from the implementation author.

## Cross receipts

Each of seven edges has a local consumer import task and producer checkpoint. Receipts contain project UUID, task/checkpoint, exact source commit, source dependencies, interface/evidence hashes and independent review. Qualified capabilities additionally identify actual nonempty tests. The helper reobserves producer completion/checkpoint through native reads. A handwritten done flag or checksum alone is insufficient.

The bootstrap verifier uses an exact producer HEAD policy for simplicity. The controller may introduce a reviewed dependency-sensitive descendant renewal once supported, but cannot silently treat stale evidence as current. Evidence belongs outside source repositories to avoid self-referential commits.
